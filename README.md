## What the Data Revealed

### Only 3.73% of Restaurants Are Top Rated
> Out of 8,680 restaurants, only **324 score above 4.5** —
> meaning 96.27% of Swiggy's vendor base falls below premium
> quality threshold, a direct input for vendor improvement programs

### Kolkata and Mumbai Dominate Restaurant Density
> Kolkata leads with **1,346 restaurants**, followed by Mumbai
> **(1,277)**, Chennai **(1,106)**, and Pune **(1,090)** —
> highest-priority markets for operational scaling and
> regional vendor onboarding

### Chinese Cuisine Leads Demand Across All Major Cities
> Chinese dominates with **2,816 restaurants** vs North Indian
> **(2,077)** and Indian **(1,934)** — appearing as the #1 food
> type across Kolkata, Mumbai, Pune, Chennai, and Hyderabad

### Rohini Leads Area-wise Density at 257 Restaurants
> Rohini **(257)**, Chembur **(208)**, and Kothrud **(149)** lead
> in restaurant density — high-traffic zones that represent
> priority areas for vendor acquisition and delivery optimization

### Higher Prices Correlate with Better Ratings AND Faster Delivery
> Positive price-rating correlation confirms premium vendors
> invest in both quality and logistics — while a **negative**
> price-delivery time correlation shows higher-priced restaurants
> deliver faster, directly informing how Swiggy structures
> premium vendor partnerships

### Delivery Time Paradox — Higher Time, Higher Rating
> Positive correlation between delivery time and rating suggests
> customers in slower-paced areas rate higher — revealing that
> delivery optimization strategy must be city-specific,
> not blanket

---

## Problem Statement

Swiggy's restaurant performance data — spanning ratings, cuisines,
pricing, and delivery times across multiple cities — existed in
raw, disconnected form. Without a unified analytical view, patterns
critical to vendor onboarding, delivery optimization, and customer
targeting remained hidden.

---

## Approach

- Loaded and profiled 8,680 records, resolving missing values
  across price, rating, and delivery time fields
- Created calculated columns for Price Range bands and
  Rating Categories to enable segmentation
- Built DAX measures for Top-Rated Spots%, Average Ratings,
  Total Restaurants, and Unique Cuisines
- Designed 3 interactive dashboards completing all 14 business
  tasks — from geographical mapping to correlation analysis
- Structured visuals to serve both strategic teams (market
  expansion, vendor decisions) and operational teams
  (delivery optimization, pricing strategy)

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

1. **Vendor Quality Program** — Target the 96.27% below 4.5
   with structured improvement initiatives
2. **Delivery Optimization** — Prioritize Kolkata, Mumbai,
   Chennai where aggregate delivery times are highest
3. **Cuisine Expansion** — Onboard trending cuisines in
   high-demand areas with limited variety
4. **Localized Marketing** — Run campaigns in Rohini, Chembur,
   Kothrud — proven high-density zones
5. **Premium Vendor Strategy** — Leverage price-rating
   correlation to structure tiered vendor partnerships
